Overview

There is a game in Krikill named Shingles, this is a board game. It has an 8 by 8 board, and you can play as either red or white. The goal is to have a piece of your side standing. All starting pieces are called "Checks". They can move diagonally but they have to kill horizontally or vertically. If a Check piece goes to a corner, it can promote to either a Fable, Capturer or to a Jumper. A Fable can move vertically or horizontally but has to capture horizontally while a Jumper can go 2 tiles diagonally and kill a piece and it can jump over over pieces. A Capturer can go 1 or 2 tiles horizontally but cannot jump over other pieces. You can kill your own pieces.
