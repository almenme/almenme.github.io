Prologue

You had just gone outside when you saw a car. It was unique, blue, and with a look so ultra-futuresque that you had to see what it would do. So, curious you had decided, for some reason to enter the car. The driver asked for a fee, you gave it and then the car started. You were initially wondering if this was a car with some decoration, but as more time passed by: The car began to become faster. You believed initially that it was simply a fast car, until it started to go to speeds that felt like it was a flicker, purely of your dreams. You were worried of a car crash and yet, it didn't, dodging every vehicle it saw. Then, tires started to burn from all the friction, flames engulfing the vehicle. You were sure you wouldn't survive. You shouted at the driver, screaming at him how much of an idiot he was, till you suddenly and unexpectedly became unconscious.

What?

You wake up in the streets, not having a clue of what happened. When you process the situation, you realise that you have really time-travelled. Right in front of you is a statue. A man riding atop a horse, and then you see the plaque, and the plaque reads: "Rogia II, patron of Iza". Up above, the sun is shining and you can feel it is early morning. You ask one of the locals, who is able to speak in English, and say "It is 7 AM as of now." You see the local, with a clear tan complexion and with clothes suited for the environment that is sunny and hot. You turn. And you see buildings, duplexes and apartment complexes with a variety of colors and classic in your eyes. And then you ask another local where you are, the local replied "In Centó Regio, to the east is Ukuma Regio, West is Resédentialé Regio, to the north is Senexlé Regio and to the south it is Mulak Regio."
