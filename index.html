Overview

Krikill is a nation with 3 chambers, making it tricarmal, the Lower House (Aslekikne), The First Chamber (Kinämlet), and the Senate (Ründajma), The Lower House is the only chamber that appoints the Executive President alongside being the chamber that passes bills, Kinämlet has the special power to force bills to a referendum and can delay bills by 120 days, and the Ründajma, are just responsible for proofreading and are appointed by the Executive President.

The Executive President is both the Head of State and the Head of Government being appointed by the Aslekikne, he can be removed with a motion of no confidence with a majority vote required.

Krikill is a multi-party system, using STV voting system with 10 seats for every constituency. It has elections every 4 years for the Lower House, 5 for the Kinämlet and the Ründajma is appointed by the Executive President, which selects 30 members to appoint, out of 200 members, with the remaining having been appointed by past Executive Presidents.

History

Origin

In 6,531 GK, following the Krikillian revolution: There were two main wings of the revolution, each with their unique bases.

Moderates—Composed of the merchants and those that had led the revolution
And Republicans—The working-class, many of those who had actively fought in the revolution and the press.

They diverged upon the structure of the parliament. The moderates supported a tricameral parliament while the Republicans wanted a unicameral parliament. The moderates had their way thanks to their political strength and assets, and got a tricameral parliament into the constitution. The Moderates became the Liberal Party and the Republicans became the Whig Party. There was also a very small faction of monarchists as the Tory party, but they had lost their sway.

First Republic

Due to the crisis between the Ründajma and Aslekikne in 6,556 GK regarding voting reforms. The Ründajma losing equal status and purely being able to delay. While universal suffrage in the Aslekikne was implemented in 6,556 GK, the Kinämlet was still based on a system that weighed heavily towards rich areas. This period from 6,531 GK to 6,632 GK is named the First Republic, which started with the Krikillian Revolution and ended with the amendment of the second article which guaranteed universal suffrage. The system was heavily weighted towards the interests of the wealthy, who blocked further reforms and the weakening of the Kinämlet for the period.
