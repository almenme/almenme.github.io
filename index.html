Prologue

You had just gone outside when you saw a car. It was unique, blue, and with a look so ultra-futuresque that you had to see what it would do. So, curious you had decided, for some reason to enter the car. The driver asked for a fee, you gave it and then the car started. You were initially wondering if this was a car with some decoration, but as more time passed by: The car began to become faster. You believed initially that it was simply a fast car, until it started to go to speeds that felt like it was a flicker, purely of your dreams. You were worried of a car crash and yet, it didn't, dodging every vehicle it saw. Then, tires started to burn from all the friction, flames engulfing the vehicle. You were sure you wouldn't survive. You shouted at the driver, screaming at him how much of an idiot he was, till you suddenly and unexpectedly became unconscious.
