Krikill is—the land of prosperity With all the safety, And filled with a variety; Of opinions to be nurtured by democracy! O shall Krikill rise, May Krikill be free from monarchy, It can and shall reach new highs! And triumph, does liberty! Krikill is—the land of prosperity With all the safety, And filled with a variety; Of opinions to be nurtured by democracy! Old things shall give way to new, Rotten institutions shall no longer rule— Krikill shall rise anew! With all the jewels!
